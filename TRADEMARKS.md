# Quipkey name and logo

Quipkey's source code is free software under the [GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE): you may use,
change and share it under that license. The license covers the code, not the name.

"Quipkey" and the Quipkey logo identify the official app from this project and
[quipkey.dev](https://quipkey.dev). Please follow these rules:

- **Allowed without asking:** talking about Quipkey, writing about it, linking to it, and saying
  that your project works with Quipkey or is based on it.
- **Allowed:** shipping the unchanged official builds, or packaging Quipkey for a Linux
  distribution with only the changes needed to package it, under the Quipkey name.
- **Not allowed without permission:** distributing a changed version under the Quipkey name or
  logo, or using the name or logo in a way that suggests the project endorses you or your
  product. If you publish a fork with your own changes, give it a different name and logo.

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
